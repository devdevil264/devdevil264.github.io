Our Latest Rescue: Baby Raccoons

Welcome to Wild Rescues. Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and release wildlife back into their natural environment.

Our staff provides around-the-clock care for wildlife in need and long-term care for animals that cannot be released.

Will you partner with us? We need volunteers, donations, and sponsors. Contact us today to help support wildlife rescue efforts.

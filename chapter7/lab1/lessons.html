Private Lessons

We make learning music fun! We offer private lessons for piano, guitar, and violin. Contact us today to schedule your first lesson for free!

We provide:

  • Dedicated and Professional Instructors
  • No Long-Term Contracts
  • Flexible Schedule
  • Spring and Winter Performances
  • Summer Camps

Piano Lessons

We offer piano lessons for all ages, beginning at age three. Whether you are a beginner or have been playing for a while, we have a program to suit your needs. Learn techniques, theory, harmony, and tone. Piano helps develop hand-eye coordination and confidence.

Guitar Lessons

We teach acoustic, electric, bass guitar, and ukulele. Lessons are available for beginners, intermediate, and advanced players. Improve your skills with weekly lessons and learn from experienced instructors. We also offer a rock band program.

Violin Lessons

We offer violin lessons for all ages, beginning at age six. Lessons include styles from fiddle to classical. Our instructors help refine technique and reach musical goals through customized learning plans.
